Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how Biedrība “UNTU” (“UNTU”, “we”) processes personal data when you visit untu.ngo, contact us, apply for our projects or donate. We process personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Latvian Personal Data Processing Law.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Biedrība “UNTU”, registration No. 40008271520, legal address Lībekas iela 6a-2, Rīga, LV-1014, Latvia.
Contact for all privacy questions: projects@untu.ngo

2. What data we process and why

  • Collaboration form on our homepage. Your role, name, email, organisation (optional) and message. We use them to reply to you and to discuss possible cooperation. Legal basis: steps at your request before entering into an agreement and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). The form is processed by Formspree, Inc. (USA) on our behalf.
  • Project applications. When you apply for a training course, youth exchange or other project, we collect the data asked in the application form (for example name, contact details, country, organisation, motivation and practical needs such as dietary requirements). We use them to select participants, organise travel, accommodation and the programme, and to report to the funding programme (Erasmus+ / the Agency for International Programs for Youth). Legal basis: preparing and performing your participation (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legal and contractual reporting obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). Information about health or dietary needs is processed only if you provide it, and only to keep you safe and comfortable during the activity (Art. 9(2)(a)). Application forms are hosted by Google (Google Forms).
  • Donations. Donations are made through PayPal. PayPal shares with us your name, email address, the amount and the date. PayPal processes your payment data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We keep donation records to meet accounting obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Emails. If you write to us, we process your message and contact details to reply (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Photos and videos from activities. We may publish photos and videos from our projects to show their results and meet the visibility rules of EU funding. We inform participants beforehand. You may ask us at any time to remove a photo of you.

3. Technical data and third‑party services on the website

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data (such as IP address, browser type and the requested page) needed to deliver the page and protect the site. Some pages load content from other providers, and your browser connects to them directly, which shares your IP address with them:

  • Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.): fonts;
  • ImageKit: images;
  • unpkg.com: the Leaflet map library;
  • CARTO and OpenStreetMap: map tiles;
  • Open‑Meteo: the weather forecast on the training pages.

Links to Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Google Docs and similar services only share data with those services after you click them.

4. Cookies

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Some project pages save your language choice in your browser’s local storage. This stays on your device, is not sent to us, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

5. How long we keep data

  • Collaboration form messages and emails: up to 12 months after our last contact, unless they lead to cooperation.
  • Project participant data: for as long as the grant agreement and audit rules of the funding programme require after the project ends.
  • Donation and accounting records: for the period required by Latvian accounting law.

After these periods we delete or anonymise the data.

6. Who receives your data

We do not sell your data. Your data are processed by the service providers listed above on our behalf. Where relevant, they are also shared with project partner organisations, accommodation and transport providers, and the funding agency. Some providers (Formspree, Google, Cloudflare, PayPal) may process data in the USA. These transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data and to ask us to correct, delete or restrict it. You can object to processing, receive your data in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before. To use any of these rights, write to projects@untu.ngo. We reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija, www.dvi.gov.lv).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page with its update date.
