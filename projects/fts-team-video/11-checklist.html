Checklist

Before you press record

Roles are clear.

Platform chosen: vertical or horizontal.

Script written, facts checked.

Camera: 4K or 1080p, 30 fps.

Mic on and tested.

Plan for 1 to 2 minutes.

Subtitles if not in English.

Rec. Film, cut, post with the caption and tags.
