Chapter 3 · People · What happened

Normal for whom?

1 · Slurping your noodles loudly

Japan: it shows you enjoy the food.

2 · Having dinner at 10 pm

Spain: a normal time for dinner.

3 · Kissing a new colleague on both cheeks

France: "la bise", a usual hello.

4 · Nodding your head to say "no"

Bulgaria: a nod can mean no, a shake can mean yes.

5 · Leaving no tip in a restaurant

Japan: tips are not expected and can even confuse people.

6 · Taking your shoes off when you visit friends

Latvia, Norway, Estonia and many more: shoes stay at the door.

"Rude" usually means: different from how we do it at home.
