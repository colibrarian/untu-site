Chapter 3 · People · In simple words

Just-world belief

We want the world to be fair. So when something bad happens to someone, we think: they must have deserved it.

Like this

"She failed the exam, she is just lazy." We did not see that she works every evening.

Why the brain does it

If people get what they deserve, then nothing bad will happen to me. It feels safe.

Today

The jury saw only the bicycles. Not the cards.

Lerner, M. J. (1980). The Belief in a Just World.
