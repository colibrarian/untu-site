Chapter 3 · People · Your team

Think of a team with roles

A project you did together, where everyone had a job. Talk in small groups:

1

Did the roles work out as planned? Who ended up doing more?

2

When someone did less, what did you think first: "lazy" or "something got in the way"?

3

What was on their "card" that you did not see?
