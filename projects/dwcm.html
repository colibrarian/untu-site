Project overview

Daily Wellness Challenge Movement

Erasmus+ KA153-YOU – Mobility of Youth Workers

🌍
The Project

Daily Wellness Challenge Movement was an Erasmus+ project bringing together youth workers from Latvia, Norway, Germany and Estonia to explore wellbeing education, digital engagement and international cooperation.

🏔️
The Training Course

The international training course took place in Vircava, Latvia. Mobility period: 21–30 October 2025, including arrival/departure days; 8-day learning programme: 22–29 October 2025. Participants explored practical approaches to wellbeing and non-formal education, including mindfulness, movement-based learning, yoga and sound-based practices, while strengthening their skills in digital storytelling and content creation.

🔄
Impact & Sustainability

The project supported the exchange of methods and good practices between participating organisations and encouraged youth workers to transfer their learning to their organisations and local communities. It also contributed to the development of practical and scalable resources that can continue to be used beyond the mobility.

Digital Awareness Campaign

Central campaign + local concepts.

As part of the follow-up process, participants and national teams were encouraged to apply the digital communication and wellness competences developed during the project to their own organisational contexts. Rather than reproducing one identical campaign in all partner countries, teams developed local concepts reflecting the needs of their audiences and regular youth-work activities.
