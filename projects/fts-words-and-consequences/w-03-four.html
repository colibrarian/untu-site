Before you speak in public

Four questions

1

Who is listening?

And how many of them?

2

What will they do

after my words?

3

Who could get hurt?

Can they answer back?

4

What do I know,

and what do I only assume?

Two things often cause harm without anyone noticing: blaming people for what happened to them, and taking our normal as the only normal.
