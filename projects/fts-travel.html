If you arrive by plane

Fly into Riga International Airport (RIX) — the closest major airport.

  • Airport → Riga centre: take bus No. 22 or the Airport Express shuttle to the Central Bus/Train Station (“Autoosta”), about 20–30 minutes. Buying an e‑ticket in advance (Narvesen kiosks or the Mobilly app) is recommended.
  • Riga → Jelgava by bus: board a Hansa Lines (or similar carrier) bus from Rīgas SAO — departs roughly every 20 minutes, ~55 minutes to Jelgava Bus Station, on average €2.80.
  • Riga → Jelgava by train: suburban electric train from the Central Railway Station, every 30–60 minutes, ~45 minutes, on average €2.80 one way, ticket at the station office or from the conductor.
  • Jelgava → Vircava by bus: route 6764 (Wanema) from Jelgava Bus Station, ~20 minutes, €1–2. Ask the driver to drop you near your accommodation.
  • Pick‑up: we meet participants at Jelgava train station and bring them to Vircava (~12 km, 15 min). Agree the time with Kate — see "Arrival day" above.

Green travel (train, bus or car)

  • Bus & train: same route as above — reach Jelgava first, then bus 6764 to Vircava.
  • Driving: Vircava is ~55 km south‑west of Riga via the A8/E77 towards Jelgava, then local roads — about 49 minutes. Parking & public transport details will be covered in the online preparatory meeting.
  • International coaches typically stop at Riga Bus Station — continue by local bus or train from there.
Please arrive at Jelgava train station on 8 November 2026, after 18:00 — training starts the next morning.

Upon arrival

  • Keep all travel receipts (plane, bus, train, fuel) for reimbursement under Erasmus+ rules.

⚠️ Do not buy tickets before approval +

Before purchasing any tickets, every participant / national team must send the complete proposed travel plan to the coordinating / hosting organisation Biedrība UNTU and receive written approval. Only after approval should tickets be purchased.

Tickets purchased without prior approval are purchased at the participant's own risk and may not be accepted for reimbursement if the route, dates, price or travel mode do not comply with the agreed project conditions.

Entry requirements for third‑country nationals

From 1 September 2025 third country nationals are obligated to submit their identification information and information on purpose and duration of travelling to Latvia. This obligation also applies to travellers transiting through Latvia.

Some third‑country nationals may be required to submit an Electronic Travel Declaration at least 48 hours before entering Latvia. Please check the official eligibility requirements through the link below.

Who needs this: only citizens of countries outside the EU / EEA, Switzerland, NATO, the OECD and Brazil who do not hold a Latvian visa or residence permit. Most participants from partner countries are EU / EEA citizens and do not need to submit anything.

Please read more about the regulation established by the Republic of Latvia here.

The traveller is obliged and responsible for providing their own data and for specifying whether they are subject to the obligation to provide their data.

The information must be submitted electronically here.

Keep your confirmation: after submitting on eta.gov.lv you receive a confirmation by e‑mail — keep it on your phone while travelling.
