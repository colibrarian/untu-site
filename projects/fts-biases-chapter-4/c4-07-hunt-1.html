Emotion, authority, personal experience, or no trick?

  1. 1. Ad: “Dr. Lisa M., nutrition expert, drinks GreenBoost every morning. Shouldn’t you?”
    → Authority
  2. 2. Charity post: “Look into her eyes. Will you scroll past again?”
    → Emotion
  3. 3. Comment: “I never vaccinated my kids and they are healthier than all their classmates.”
    → Personal experience
  4. 4. Politician: “As a mother of three, I know what is best for every family in this country.”
    → Personal experience + authority
  5. 5. Ad: “The app 9 out of 10 influencers use. Don’t be the one left behind!”
    → Authority + emotion

Decide first, then click to see the answer. Some have two tricks, one has none.
