Chapter 4 · Persuasion

Appeal to emotion

Instead of reasons, the speaker gives you fear, pity, anger or delight.

How it works

A strong feeling switches System 2 off. We decide first and look for reasons later.

Example

An ad for a home alarm shows a dark house and a scared family at night. Not one number on how often break-ins happen.

Plays on: availability (ch. 1), and negativity (ch. 3).
