Teleology

The belief that everything happens for a purpose. Like "new" and "old", it sounds like a reason without being one.

How it works

We explain events by their goal instead of their cause. It feels like an explanation, but it adds an intention nobody had.

Example

"It rains so that plants can grow." "Rocks are pointy so that animals can scratch on them." "This happened to me so that I would learn something."

Kelemen, D. (1999). Why are rocks pointy? Children's preference for teleological explanations.
