“Close the youth centre on weekends”

Friends, I will be short, because the facts speak for themselves. Every single person I talked to this week agrees with me. Last Saturday a window was broken at our youth centre. Next time it could be a child who gets hurt. Can you live with that? My own nephew went to the centre on Saturdays for a year, and his grades went down. So we know what weekends there do to young people. A famous football coach said on TV last month that young people need structure, not free time. If he says it, it is true. Some people say the centre costs only €2,000 a year to stay open on weekends. Others say €20,000. Let us be realistic and say it is closer to €20,000. There are only two options. Either we close the centre on weekends, or we accept vandalism in our town. The town next to ours closed its centre on weekends five years ago, and they have had no problems since. And remember: our grandparents grew up without youth centres at all, and they turned out fine. So let us do the responsible thing. Thank you.

One person reads, everyone else keeps a tally of tricks.
