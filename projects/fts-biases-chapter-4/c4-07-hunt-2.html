Emotion, authority, personal experience, or no trick?

  1. 6. Post: “If you really cared about animals, you would share this. Only monsters stay silent.”
    → Emotion (shame)
  2. 7. Speech: “A famous actor told me our town is the most dangerous in the country.”
    → Authority
  3. 8. News: “The city council will vote on the new bus routes on Thursday. The plan is published on the council website.”
    → No trick
  4. 9. Post: “Studies show that people who drink coffee every day are more successful. Join them.”
    → Authority: which studies? no source. And correlation is not cause
  5. 10. Interview: “I have taught for 30 years, and I can tell you: this new reading app does not work. I tried it once with my class.”
    → Authority + personal experience: one class, one try

Decide first, then click to see the answer. Some have two tricks, one has none.
