From Thinking to Speaking● The order

The order

Usually theory first. Here, the other way round

Usual
Theory → Practice → Test
This week
Experiment → What happened? → Conclusions → The idea gets a name → Use it in your life
Why

With cognitive biases, theory first breaks the experiment. Once you know the trick, it stops working on you. So you fall for it first, and then we look at what happened.

Result

The idea is not something you were told. It is something that happened to you, in this room.

From Thinking to Speaking● Conclusions

The part we never skip

Every activity ends with conclusions

1

What happened?

Facts first. What did we do, what did we see?

2

What did I notice?

In me, in the group, in the result.

3

What does it mean?

Now the idea, the name, the research.

4

What will I do?

Where will I see it again in my life?

Why 1

People feel heard. What they lived and said becomes part of the result.

Why 2

Each person builds their own logical chain: from the event, to what happened, to the conclusion they reached.
