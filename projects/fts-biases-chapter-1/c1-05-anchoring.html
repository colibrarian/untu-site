Anchoring

The first number we hear becomes the starting point, even when it is obviously random.

How it works

One half was asked "Are there more or fewer than 2,000 McDonald's restaurants in the world?", the other "more or fewer than 200,000?". Both anchors are far off, yet the average guesses split apart. There are about 43,000.

Example

"Was €200, now €99." The crossed-out price is the anchor that makes €99 feel cheap.
