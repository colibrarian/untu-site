Priming

What we saw or heard first quietly changes how we read what comes next.

How it works

The brain prepares for what it expects. A word, a picture or a headline switches on related ideas, and new information gets read through them.

Try it

Say "white" five times, out loud. Now: what do cows drink? Most people say "milk". Cows drink water. A headline does the same to the article below it.
