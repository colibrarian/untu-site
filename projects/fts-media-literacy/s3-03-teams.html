True, False, Framed Session 3 · Confirmation bias & young people

Three positions

Three answers to one problem

Team A

Protect: limit access

Age limits, phone-free schools, filters. Young people can’t fight an algorithm built by thousands of engineers, so adults must close the door. Australia already did it.

Team B

Trust: open access

Young people have the same right to information as adults. Bans push them to hidden corners. Give them skills and trust, not locks.

Team C

Fix the feed, not the kids

The problem is the design. Platforms must change their algorithms, show other views and be open about why you see what you see.
