True, False, Framed Session 3 · Confirmation bias & young people

Why it matters for young people

The feed learns what you already believe

  • 01You stop on a post that agrees with you. The feed notices.
  • 02It shows you more of the same, and fewer posts that don’t fit.
  • 03Your friends see their own version. Everyone feels sure.
  • 04For teenagers it also decides who “people like me” are.

How strong filter bubbles are is still debated in research. That feeds reward attention, and that agreeing content gets attention, is not.
