SIFT: four moves, a few minutes

S

Stop

Strong feeling? Pause before you believe or share.

I

Investigate the source

New tab: who is behind it, and what do they gain?

F

Find better coverage

Do trusted sources report the same? Or nothing at all?

T

Trace to the original

Follow the quote, number or photo back to where it started.
