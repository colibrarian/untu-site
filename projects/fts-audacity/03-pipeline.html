From Thinking to Speaking · Podcast editing in Audacity Intro

Workflow roadmap

Complete episode production cycle

Step 01

Import & rough cut

Trim dead air, stuttering, false starts, and breathing artifacts.

Step 02

Noise reduction

Capture ambient room noise profiles and apply non-destructive reduction.

Step 03

Tonal balancing (EQ)

High-pass filter low rumble and boost vocal presence.

Step 04

Compression

Compress dynamic spikes so loud and quiet passages sit closer together.

Step 05

Loudness & export

Normalize to platform loudness targets and export the final MP3.
