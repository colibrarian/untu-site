Stage 02

Noise reduction: the two-step profile method

Step 1 · Noise profile acquisition

Highlight 1–2 seconds of pure ambient room silence (no vocal activity).

Effect → Noise Removal and Repair → Noise Reduction → Get Noise Profile

Step 2 · Full track processing

Select the entire track (Ctrl + A Cmd + A), then apply with:

  • Noise reductionavoids metallic/robotic phase artifacts10–12 dB
  • Sensitivity6.00
  • Frequency smoothing3 bands
